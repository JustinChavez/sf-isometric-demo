The model

Fine-tuned on Qwen Image Edit

The current map was generated with Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 — a ~20B image-editing model — fine-tuned as a LoRA on about 250 render-to-pixel-art pairs (50 city views × 5 training variants) at 1024 px, trained to ~2,750 steps. Every tile is then generated with that LoRA attached.

Experiments

Cheaper generation

Today a tile takes 25 steps for full quality. Fewer steps are cheaper but quality drops. The goal is a Turbo/Lightning LoRA or Krea 2 style transfer that keeps quality at the low step count.

The map

A Coenen-style San Francisco

A city-wide map in the same spirit as Andy Coenen’s Isometric NYC — whose launch post drew 7,800+ likes. Full SF is about 14,400 tiles: 12 km × 12 km at 100 m pitch is 120 × 120.
